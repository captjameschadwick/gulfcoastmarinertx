About Us

We provide hands‑on, on‑water instruction for new boaters across the Texas Gulf Coast. Every session is designed to build confidence, improve safety awareness, and teach real navigation skills. Whether you're brand new or sharpening your abilities, Captain James delivers patient, professional guidance tailored to your experience level.

Meet the Captain

Captain James Chadwick is a 100‑Ton U.S. Coast Guard Master Captain with over 25 years of professional maritime experience. His career spans multiple sectors of the industry, giving him a depth of knowledge that few recreational instructors can match.

For more than a decade, James served as a supply boat and crew boat captain, operating vessels ranging from 110 ft to 200 ft throughout the Gulf of Mexico. His time offshore built a foundation of real-world seamanship, vessel handling, navigation, and safety awareness that he now brings to every training session.

James also spent several years running as a captain for the Port Aransas Ferry System, safely transporting thousands of passengers and vehicles across challenging waterways. This role demanded precision, calm decision-making, and mastery of close‑quarters maneuvering — skills he now teaches to new boaters with patience and clarity.

In addition to his commercial experience, James works operational shifts with Sea Tow, giving him firsthand knowledge of emergency response, vessel recovery, and on‑water safety. His calm teaching style, deep local knowledge, and commitment to empowering new boaters make him one of the most trusted instructors in the Coastal Bend.

Whether you're brand new to boating or looking to sharpen your navigation skills, Captain James delivers professional, confidence‑building instruction tailored to your experience level.
