About Us

We provide hands‑on, on‑water instruction for new boaters across the Texas Gulf Coast. Every session is designed to build confidence, improve safety awareness, and teach real navigation skills. Whether you're brand new or sharpening your abilities, Captain James delivers patient, professional guidance tailored to your experience level.

Training Services

New Boater Instruction

Hands‑on training covering boat handling, throttle control, docking, situational awareness, and comfort on the water.

Navigation Trips

Real‑world navigation practice including channels, markers, shallow water, route planning, and safe travel techniques.

Safety Training

Essential safety skills: emergency procedures, weather awareness, equipment use, and risk reduction on the water.
