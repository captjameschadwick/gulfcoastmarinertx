About Captain James Chadwick Sr.

Captain James Chadwick Sr. is a USCG 100 Ton Master with deep roots in the Gulf Coast. He brings years of real-world operational experience across the Coastal Bend, supporting vessel movements, marine logistics, and safe boating practices for clients and crews.

Known for his calm, patient instruction style, James helps new boaters build confidence on the water while providing dependable support for families, charters, and marine projects. His work reflects a commitment to safety, professionalism, and clear communication in every operation.

Gulf Coast Mariner Services & Logistics represents James’s dedication to the waterways he grew up on—offering reliable captain services, hands-on training, and trustworthy marine support throughout the Gulf Coast region.
