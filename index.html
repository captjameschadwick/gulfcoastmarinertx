About Us

We provide hands‑on, on‑water instruction for new boaters across the Texas Gulf Coast. Every session is designed to build confidence, improve safety awareness, and teach real navigation skills.

Meet the Captain

Captain James Chadwick is a U.S. Coast Guard 100‑Ton Master Captain with more than 25 years of experience on the water. He’s spent his career operating a wide range of vessels across the Texas Gulf Coast and beyond.

James spent over a decade running 110–200 ft supply boats and crew boats in the Gulf of Mexico, gaining real‑world knowledge in vessel handling, navigation, and safety. He also served as a captain for the Port Aransas Ferry System, where close‑quarters maneuvering and precision were part of every shift.

Today, James continues to work operational shifts with Sea Tow, adding emergency response and vessel recovery experience to his background. His calm teaching style and deep familiarity with local waters make him a trusted instructor for new boaters looking to learn safely and confidently.

Training Services

New Boater Instruction

Hands‑on training covering boat handling, throttle control, docking, situational awareness, and comfort on the water.

Navigation Trips

Real‑world navigation practice including channels, markers, shallow water, route planning, and safe travel techniques.

Safety Training

Essential safety skills: emergency procedures, weather awareness, equipment use, and risk reduction on the water.

Texas Boater Safety Certification Classes

State‑approved boater education covering legal requirements, safe operation, navigation rules, and essential boating knowledge needed to earn your Texas Boater Safety Certification.
